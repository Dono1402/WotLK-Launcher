# Atlas Launcher 1.7.2

Prepared release, awaiting publication and activation on the realm.

- Convert an offline character’s gold into Atlas credits: 100 gold grants
  €1 in credits. Remaining silver and copper stay on the character.
- Track the conversion and receive a receipt after realm confirmation.
  Recover a lost response without spending the gold twice.
- Use Atlas credits to buy a name change. Choose the character and its new
  name through the native service in game.
- The euro wallet remains separate. Pending conversions and their results
  are recovered after refreshing or signing back into the launcher.

Conversion is final. Keep every character on the account logged out while it
is processed. This release does not enable other services or PayPal payments.
