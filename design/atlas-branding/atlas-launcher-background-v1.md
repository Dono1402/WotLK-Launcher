# Atlas Launcher — fond multiservice, proposition 1

Visuel généré le 26 septembre 2026 avec l’outil de génération d’images intégré à Codex.

Fichier : `atlas-launcher-background-v1.png`.

Cette proposition associe un nouvel emblème A orbital, le nom Atlas Launcher et quatre personnages : Goku, Luffy, Gojo et Frieren. La partie sombre à gauche réserve de la place pour le formulaire. Le logo et le nom sont intégrés à l’image : une éventuelle intégration devra éviter de superposer le logo actuel à cette identité.

Il s’agit d’une proposition graphique conservée séparément du fond utilisé par le launcher. Elle n’est pas encore raccordée à l’interface.

## Prompt de génération

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: polished full-bleed background artwork for the Atlas Launcher desktop sign-in screen, a future multi-service platform for games and anime. Generate ONE finished landscape illustration, ideally 2560 x 1600, approximately 16:10 aspect ratio.
Primary request: a beautiful branded background with an Atlas emblem, the exact name ATLAS LAUNCHER, and several recognizable anime characters. This is the artwork itself, NOT a UI mockup, not a screenshot, not a poster displayed inside a room.
Composition: deliberately asymmetric. Keep the left 38% predominantly quiet, dark midnight navy, with a refined atmospheric gradient and very restrained texture so real login controls can later be overlaid. Put a carefully designed luminous silver and ice-blue geometric A emblem, a tasteful subtle orbital arc around it, and the crisp exact wordmark "ATLAS" with smaller widely spaced "LAUNCHER" beneath it, in the upper-left quadrant, around x=8%-34%, y=12%-27%. Treat this as an elegant coherent platform brand, not a Warcraft crest. Brand text must be perfectly spelled, clean and readable. The left area below the branding must remain free of characters, text, decorative panels or bright features.
On the right 60%, create an intentional cinematic ensemble of FOUR clearly recognizable anime characters: adult Son Goku with black spiky hair and orange gi, Monkey D. Luffy with his straw hat and red open jacket, Satoru Gojo with white hair and black blindfold and dark high-collar outfit, and Frieren with long silver hair, pointed elf ears and her white and gold mage outfit. Stylish confident relaxed poses, upper-body to three-quarter framing, distinct faces and silhouettes, natural anatomy, no duplicated characters. Layer them in a visually balanced group rather than four separate rectangular portraits. Vary the heights and depths, keep every face fully visible and within safe image bounds, retain their iconic character designs and each character's own color accents.
Scene: one unified sophisticated anime fantasy world with a distant luminous city and soft clouds, subtle celestial arcs suggesting different worlds meeting, a few floating lights. Rich layered painterly anime backgrounds and exceptionally crisp hand-drawn character linework and elegant cel shading. Premium anime key visual, visually coherent, meticulous faces and hands. The artwork fades naturally into the quiet dark left section without a hard split.
Lighting and palette: deep navy and indigo foundation, controlled cyan rim lights and soft violet atmosphere, balanced warm orange and red from the characters, gentle warm highlights. Atmospheric and welcoming rather than aggressive; avoid excessive neon, lightning or visual clutter.
Constraints: only text is "ATLAS" and "LAUNCHER", no other words, no slogans, no franchise logos. No actual form fields, buttons, cursor, borders, panels, watermark or UI chrome. No Icecrown citadel or Warcraft scenery. No sexualization or violence. Keep the rightmost upper corner relatively calm for eventual window controls. Make the illustration feel like a polished desktop platform identity with real usable negative space, not a collage of unrelated screenshots.
```
