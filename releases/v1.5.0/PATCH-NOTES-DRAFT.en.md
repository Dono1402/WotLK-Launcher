# Atlas Launcher 1.5.0

Atlas messaging arrives with private and group conversations, file sharing and integrated players. This release also improves presence, friend profiles and conversations with players in game, and fixes avatar cropping.

Publication in preparation.

## Messages and conversations

- New Messages page with a conversation list, contact search, an Unread filter and a + button to start a conversation.
- Private conversations and groups with names, pictures, invitations and member management. New members can access history from the time they join the group.
- Reply to messages, react with emoji, edit and delete your own messages, and pin conversations and messages.
- Messages grouped by author and day, a new-message marker, and typing and read indicators that respect the selected presence status.
- Text formatting supports bold, italic, lists, quotes, code and spoilers. Text remains selectable and can be copied.
- Drafts are saved per account and conversation. Pending sends can be resumed or cancelled after an interruption.
- A failed send can be deleted without returning after reconnection. Deleted messages also disappear from previews, replies and pins.
- Previously archived conversations remain available in All.

## Images and attachments

- Add files through the file picker or drag and drop, and paste screenshots, with a preview before sending.
- Send images, personal GIFs, documents, audio files and videos, with up to 500 MB per file and ten attachments per message.
- Images and GIFs keep their proportions, including portrait formats, without a file card around the image.
- Transfer progress, cancellation and retry. Draft previews remain stable while typing.
- An enlarged viewer and a Save as… context menu for supported images, audio files and documents.
- Links display previews. A non-video page preview can be removed without deleting the link or message.

## Audio and video

- Integrated players with play/pause, seeking, time, volume, an enlarged view and video fullscreen, both in conversations and drafts.
- Video controls appear as a transparent overlay and automatically hide during playback.
- Opening and closing the enlarged view preserves playback position and volume. Incoming messages no longer recreate the player.
- Playback continues when the launcher loses focus or is minimized. It stops when leaving Messages or switching conversations.
- YouTube and Vimeo previews start from their thumbnail. Videos near the visible area prepare their preview earlier.
- Broader audio and video attachment support, including M4A, FLAC, MOV and MKV. Integrated playback depends on the codec inside the file.

## Presence and notifications

- Online, Away, Do not disturb and Appear offline statuses can be selected from the profile menu and are shown on the avatar.
- Automatically switches to Away after twenty minutes of Windows inactivity and returns to Online when activity resumes. Manually selected statuses are preserved.
- Status is shared across Messages, the friends list and profiles. Another active session prevents you from incorrectly appearing away.
- Do not disturb suppresses message and friend notifications. Messaging sounds remain disabled.
- Appear offline hides launcher and game activity, along with shared typing and read indicators.

## Profiles, armory and avatars

- Friend profiles open in the full view, with their characters, equipment and 3D armory available to browse.
- Share a character’s Armory lets you select your character directly in Messages. The card opens the exact character that was shared.
- Fixed avatar cropping on displays using Windows scaling: the result now matches the selected area.
- Sharper profile pictures in full profiles and more reliable avatar loading in Messages and the friends list.

## Whispers with the game

- Private conversations connect the launcher with characters who are online in game.
- A message sent from the launcher appears under the name Nickname#Launcher to identify its author.
- Replies addressed to this identity return to the account’s conversation in Messages.

## Account, settings and addons while playing

- Your profile, account management, security settings and sessions remain accessible while the game is running.
- Settings and addon management remain available while playing, including installing, updating and removing addons.

## Smoothness and polish

- Smoother Messages menus, transitions and previews, while respecting the Windows reduced-motion setting.
- Improved Messages rendering cadence on high-refresh-rate displays when supported by the Windows version.
- Labels, playback controls and new states are available in French and English.
