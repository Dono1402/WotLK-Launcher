# Atlas Launcher 1.8.0

Your games, your services, one Atlas account. A new identity and a smoother sign-in experience.

## New Atlas identity

- Sign-in and registration feature the new Atlas artwork with Homura, Mitsuha, Rem and Sinon.
- The Atlas symbol becomes the launcher and installer icon. The publisher shown in Windows becomes Atlas.

## Sign-in and registration

- Click anywhere inside a field to start typing.
- Show or hide passwords with the eye button. Labels and tabs are easier to read.
- Empty fields no longer display red error messages. Incorrect entries still receive specific feedback.
- Account creation displays a confirmation and an email verification reminder.
- Forgot your password? Request a single-use reset link by email, valid for 30 minutes.

## Shop

- Automatic order updates no longer interrupt selections or ongoing input.
- Text, lists and gold conversion presentation are consistent in French and English.
